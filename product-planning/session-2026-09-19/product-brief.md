# OpenWindow: consolidated product direction

Working synthesis, 2026-09-20. Reflects the interview so far; not a final approved specification. OpenWindow remains the name from the original thesis.

## Vision

Help any group turn “let’s meet” into a suitable date, a suitable place, and an agreed plan with less coordination effort.

## Audience and market

Any group arranging a meetup: friends, families, colleagues, or communities. Start in Tashkent, with Uzbekistan as the broader intended market. Holiday planning was the original example; the audience is broader than the founder's own group. Initial outing categories remain open.

The first version supports Uzbek and Russian in its interface and AI conversation.

## Initial experience

A person initiates planning in a Telegram Mini App. Participants provide availability for this meetup. The product supports at least two paths:

- **Known place:** coordinate around a destination already chosen.
- **AI assistance:** ask three questions, then search online for suitable options. Preferences, recent meetups, and availability inform recommendations. Social media, including Instagram, are desired discovery sources; access and coverage remain unvalidated.

Decision-making remains flexible between group voting and initiator selection. Detailed confirmation rules are open.

No venue booking in the first versions: participants handle reservations. The product confirms an agreed meetup plan, not a reservation.

## Core value

Choose dates and places that work better for participants. Help people avoid forgetting meetups; reminders are a tentative supporting feature.

## Platform progression

- First: Telegram Mini App.
- Possible companion: a bot added to Telegram groups.
- Later: standalone web app.
- Possible later integration: calendars. Initial availability is entered per meetup.
- Future feature candidate: venue booking, explicitly deferred from the first versions.

## Key open decisions

Who answers the three questions; question content; preference storage; history collection; group membership; handling passive participants; final confirmation rules; venue source feasibility; resources and timing.

## Launch economics

Start free to users. Monetization and AI spending decisions are explicitly deferred. No paid features, AI budget, or provider has been selected. Tracking pilot usage and cost is a proposed way to inform those later decisions.

## Proposed next steps

Define remaining scope, team capacity, and target first-test date; prototype both main workflows in Uzbek and Russian; test with multiple independent groups in Tashkent; and validate local search quality. Measure date/place suitability, effort to reach agreement, and completed meetups. No implementation schedule or long-term business model is committed yet.
